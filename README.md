# "The Cinderstar"
You are an agent helper sent by the ███ to investigate a parasite and surreal world in our planet.
With this in mind, you will be sent to the place of the occurrence to interview Schell, an actress who doesn't look like a conventional actress at all...
Interview her as a "behind the scenes" broadcast, try to get information from the place, and perhaps...influence her performance, for better or worse.

[Play it here!](https://ramelden.itch.io/the-cinderstar)
